How Is the Discovery Prize Created, Funded, and Distributed?

  • Funding boosted by Discovery Brick Coin (D.B.C.), backed by real estate.
  • Distributed through the Discovery Marketplace — 50/25/25.
  • Governed by the Constitution, rotating board, arbitration court, Columbo and Pipitos.
